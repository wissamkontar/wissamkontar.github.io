WK
Principal Investigator
Prof. Wissam Kontar
Assistant Professor · Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering · University of Nebraska-Lincoln

Wissam holds a Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from the University of Wisconsin-Madison (2022), co-advised by Prof. Soyoung Ahn and Prof. Andrea Hicks. He earned his B.E. from the American University of Beirut. His research focuses on operational analysis and AI applications for emerging transportation technologies, addressing challenges in automation, connectivity, and the complex behaviors of adoption ecosystems. The ultimate goal is to design and deploy technologies that improve operational performance and align with societal needs.
